FAQ

Got a question?

Answers to the questions we hear most often. For anything else, the assistant is here.

How long does it take to get started?

After a free first call, we set up the assistant with you: your services, prices and hours. Allow a few days depending on your business.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Everything runs online, from a phone or a computer.

Does the assistant send messages on my behalf?

No. PONO drafts the replies and follow-ups, and you decide whether to send them. You stay in control.

Which languages does PONO support?

English and French. The assistant can also reply in your customer's language.

How much does it cost?

USD 89 per month for Starter, USD 179 for Pro, or a custom quote from USD 320.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, plans have no commitment. You stop at the end of the current month.
